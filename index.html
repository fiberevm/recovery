RPC settings

Your private holdings are scanned as soon as the backup unlocks, using these servers. They see that someone read Fiber's pool history, never your keys or which funds are yours. Replace any of them with your own before unlocking.

This page is now limited to these servers. Reload it to change them.

Choose what you want to recover.

Private holdings

Fiber keeps private balances in its own pools on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base and BNB Chain, built on Railgun. From the outside, nobody can tell which money in a pool is yours. This page downloads each pool's public history and uses keys from your backup to find and add up your share, here in your browser.

Most of that history is saved in this page, so the servers (RPCs) chosen before unlocking are only asked for recent blocks. To use different servers, reload the page and open RPC settings.

Private key recovery

Every address in your Fiber wallet is created from your backup, one numbered step at a time. Paste one of your addresses and this page finds the step that made it, then shows that address's private key. You can import the key into another wallet, such as MetaMask for EVM addresses or Phantom for Solana.

This works offline. Anyone who sees the key can take everything at that address, so never share it.

Your backup and password never leave this browser. The page only connects to your chosen RPCs, and to ipfs-lb.com for proving files when a send needs one that isn't built in.
