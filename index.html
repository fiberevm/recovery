Recover your wallet

Fiber Recovery lets you recover your assets if Fiber.so goes down. Open your Fiber backup to see your private balance, send it out, and recover your keys. Your file and password never leave this browser.

This code is open source and can be downloaded at github.com/fiberevm/recovery.

RPC settings

Your balance is read from these servers as soon as the backup unlocks, one URL per line. They see your IP address and that someone read Fiber's pool history, never your keys or which funds are yours. Replace any of them with your own before unlocking.

This page is now limited to these servers. Reload it to change them.

Private keys

Paste one of your Fiber wallet addresses to get its private key, which you can import into another wallet such as MetaMask or Phantom. This works offline.

Anyone who sees the key can take everything at that address, so never share it.

Your backup and password never leave this browser. The page only connects to your chosen RPCs, and to ipfs-lb.com for proving files when a send needs one that isn't built in. Dollar values are estimates from on-chain price feeds, read through the same RPCs.
